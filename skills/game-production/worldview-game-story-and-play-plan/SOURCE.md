# Authorship

This method, its planning format, and The Borrowed Kite example were written for Worldview Skills. The design problem is the gap between an appealing story outline and a game whose exploration, decisions, surprises, and duration have been worked out before implementation.

The package adds scene-level writing, activity-based time estimates, early-solver and missed-clue routes, and an explicit story-first handoff. It complements the repository's narrative and production entries. It contains no third-party story, game map, dialogue, media, code, or copied Skill.

Its timings are illustrative planning estimates. Neither the example nor the method claims empirical playtest results. Research used for a creator's future project belongs in that project's source record, with factual support distinguished from invention.
