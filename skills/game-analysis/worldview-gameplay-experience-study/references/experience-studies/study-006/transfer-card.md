# Transfer card — late clarity makes information responsibility visible

`study_id: study-006`
`tags: surveillance, visual reliability, bright exposure, finale compression`
`confidence: medium`

## Keep the relationship

Late-game clarity can increase danger when the player sees more systems but must decide which representation to trust and act in a compressed route.

## Replace the surface

- setting and architecture: a floodlit offshore station with wet steel, glass, radar screens, and one catwalk
- player role and objective: systems operator isolating a failing beacon
- threat or opposing system: delayed or misleading monitor state plus exposed traversal
- visual language: clinical light, reflections, warning bands, and readable physical anchors
- sound language: radar ping, relay click, wind through steel, and a stable direction cue
- object/resource language: monitor, access badge, catwalk gate, manual override

## Build sequence

1. **Setup the expectation:** teach the player to compare a monitor with a physical door or floor.
2. **Place the readable affordance:** show a monitor prediction and the physical anchor that can correct it.
3. **Introduce the uncertainty or cost:** visibility improves while concealment disappears.
4. **Let the player choose:** trust the screen, verify the route, or spend time on a manual check.
5. **Deliver the consequence:** one preview is delayed; the physical state reveals the correction.
6. **Allow the player to explain the outcome:** the player can name the prediction, correction cue, and route.
7. **Carry the learned rule forward:** compress the final graph and use learned timing rather than adding a new maze.

## Failure boundary

Distortion without an anchor is spectacle. If every channel lies at once, the player cannot learn responsibility and failure feels arbitrary.

## First-time-player test

- Before the beat, what does the tester predict?
- What do they look at, listen for, or remember?
- What choice do they make without a tutorial prompt?
- After a failure, can they explain what they will change?
- After success, can they explain why the route worked?
