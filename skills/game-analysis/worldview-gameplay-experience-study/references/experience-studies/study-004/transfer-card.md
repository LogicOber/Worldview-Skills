# Transfer card — bright exposure can replace darkness as the threat

`study_id: study-004`
`tags: outdoor exposure, light, recurring figure, landmark loop`
`confidence: medium`

## Keep the relationship

Visibility can increase fear when it reveals the player's exposure and forces a landmark-based crossing instead of concealing the threat.

## Replace the surface

- setting and architecture: a floodlit weather yard with a pump tower and maintenance shelter
- player role and objective: surveyor returning a damaged sensor
- threat or opposing system: a distant figure and a search pattern that becomes legible only through landmarks
- visual language: rain glare, wet tile, hard floodlight, and one high-contrast pump tower
- sound language: wind, fence rattle, pump cycle, and footsteps whose distance is uncertain
- object/resource language: signal flare, shelter latch, sensor case

## Build sequence

1. **Setup the expectation:** show the pump tower and shelter before the crossing.
2. **Place the readable affordance:** give the player edges and a return landmark, not a guaranteed safe room.
3. **Introduce the uncertainty or cost:** light removes concealment while glare makes distance ambiguous.
4. **Let the player choose:** cross directly, detour to the shelter, or wait for the pump cycle.
5. **Deliver the consequence:** a recurring figure appears near a fence, then the landmark closes the loop.
6. **Allow the player to explain the outcome:** the player can identify what changed at the pump rather than merely escaping noise.
7. **Carry the learned rule forward:** later outdoor crossings add a new question or landmark.

## Failure boundary

An unmarked search that only adds walking time becomes atmosphere without play. Brightness must remove concealment or change route choice.

## First-time-player test

- Before the beat, what does the tester predict?
- What do they look at, listen for, or remember?
- What choice do they make without a tutorial prompt?
- After a failure, can they explain what they will change?
- After success, can they explain why the route worked?
