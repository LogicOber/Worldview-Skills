# Transfer card — attention is a resource shared by notes and movement

`study_id: study-005`
`tags: attention budget, notes, vertical risk, bright/dark contrast`
`confidence: medium`

## Keep the relationship

When reading and movement compete for the same attention, a partial clue can be more playable than a complete document and brightness can expose rather than reassure.

## Replace the surface

- setting and architecture: hospital records room, glass corridor, and stair landing
- player role and objective: night archivist carrying a timing-sensitive sample
- threat or opposing system: a vulnerable sightline and a patrol that punishes tunnel vision
- visual language: paper under cold task light, reflective glass, colored pipe anchor
- sound language: page scrape, ventilation pulse, distant cart, and one stable landing hum
- object/resource language: report, sample case, shutter, hand light

## Build sequence

1. **Setup the expectation:** put the actionable phrase beside a visible doorway.
2. **Place the readable affordance:** make one phrase sufficient to form a timing hypothesis.
3. **Introduce the uncertainty or cost:** reading pauses movement and hides peripheral changes.
4. **Let the player choose:** finish the report, take the phrase, or abandon the clue.
5. **Deliver the consequence:** a floor break removes the horizon and the bright corridor removes concealment.
6. **Allow the player to explain the outcome:** the pipe anchor and patrol timing show the reason for each choice.
7. **Carry the learned rule forward:** later information remains optional but actionable.

## Failure boundary

Do not make every sentence mandatory or every bright space a punishment. The player must be able to state what they chose not to attend to.

## First-time-player test

- Before the beat, what does the tester predict?
- What do they look at, listen for, or remember?
- What choice do they make without a tutorial prompt?
- After a failure, can they explain what they will change?
- After success, can they explain why the route worked?
