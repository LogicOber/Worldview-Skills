# Why procedural horror fails

## The role is a costume
If the player never performs a task, authorization and responsibility are flavor text. Teach one real procedure and use its state later.

## The procedure is a fetch list
A checklist without authority, evidence, dependency, or later payoff is walking between markers. Every task must own a route, affordance, relationship, evidence rule, promise, or resource.

## The incident pauses the job
When all deadlines and dependents disappear during the scare, duty was only an introduction. Snapshot live work and decide what continues, transfers, commits, or fails.

## “Investigate” has no verb
The player needs a concrete action: measure, compare, photograph, isolate, repair, call, carry, stop, or return. Watching a reveal is not procedural ownership.

## Authority equals innocence
A legitimate actor can misuse access; an unauthorized action can preserve life. Model permissions, evidence, and culpability separately.

## Procedure is always correct
Then there is no judgment under pressure. Make formal procedure stronger in custody or legitimacy but slower, narrower, or dependent on a compromised channel.

## Improvisation is always heroic
Improvisation should create a real cost: broken custody, exposure, consumed resource, lost trust, or uncertain proof.

## Retreat is treated as cowardice
Retreat/report can preserve knowledge and safety while costing time, access, work, or dependent protection. It must be a designed information strategy.

## Partial work silently resets
Unclear interruption makes the player repeat labor without understanding. Persist stable commits, revert only unstable motion, and show who owns the task.

## A helper erases player agency
If an ally completes the job, retain assignment, last-known state, outcome, evidence, and consequence. The player must decide the transfer and use its result.

## The witness is invisible
Later accusation based on an unshown observer is author-only knowledge. Record sightline, attention, confidence, and a cue that someone could have seen.

## The monster discards the profession
If learned tools, routes, records, and permissions stop mattering after danger appears, the opening was disposable. The final plan should convert professional knowledge into resistance or proof.

## Minimal acceptance test
A player can explain their normal duty, why they approached, what remained live, which response they chose, what work and evidence persisted, who witnessed it, how they recovered, and how the role changed by the ending.
