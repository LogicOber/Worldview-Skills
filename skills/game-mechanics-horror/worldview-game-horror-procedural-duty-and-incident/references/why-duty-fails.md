# Why procedural horror fails

- **Job without authority:** if the player has no reason to act, inspection is a cutscene.
- **Checklist as tutorial tax:** teach only the actions that later matter under pressure.
- **Incident with no evidence:** a loud accident is not an investigation; expose a detail the player can verify.
- **One correct response:** let procedure, improvisation, and retreat trade different costs while preserving the canonical ending.
- **Responsibility disappears:** after the incident, keep the job active but change what “doing it correctly” means.
