# Example: The Night Inspector

An original fictional example. Invoke it with `/worldview-game-horror-procedural-duty-and-incident` when a filled incident contract is useful. A road inspector checks three lamps and photographs each serial plate. A fourth lamp is lit but its plate is on the inside of the pole. Following procedure opens the service gate; improvising with a mirror reveals a second road; retreating records the anomaly but lets the rain erase one clue. All responses eventually lead to the same bridge, but the player reaches it with different evidence and route pressure.
