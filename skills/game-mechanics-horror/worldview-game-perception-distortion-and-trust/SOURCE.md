## Original design question

The method asks how a game can make perception unreliable without making the rules arbitrary or exploiting real mental illness as a horror label.

The answer developed here is a two-layer contract: authoritative world truth remains stable while only declared presentation channels may drift. The player retains accessible anchors, learns the scope of the distortion, and chooses whether to verify, recover, or accept risk.

## Authored decisions

- classifying presentation channels as stable, noisy, substituted, suppressed, recovery-only, or forbidden;
- requiring at least one accessible anchor for each necessary decision;
- using an escalation budget and hysteresis rather than stacking uncontrolled effects;
- keeping platform messages, saves, accessibility UI, and consent boundaries outside the fiction;
- requiring deterministic reproduction of active substitutions across save/load;
- treating assistance as an alternative expression of the mechanic rather than invalid play;
- using precise fictional causes instead of defaulting to stigmatizing “insanity.”

These are original design proposals, not clinical claims or measured statements about all players.
