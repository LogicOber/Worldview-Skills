# Source and originality record

## Commission

`worldview-game-driving-horror-divided-attention` was authored for the Worldview Skills repository as an original, all-English method for horror play inside a controlled moving vehicle.

## Method source

The method was distilled from the user's private qualitative research into recurring design problems: driving and cabin inspection can compete unfairly, stopping often lacks rules, threat behavior can ignore motion state, and comfort settings can accidentally remove the central decision. No private source is identified here.

## Strict non-copy boundary

No external title, creator, platform, plot, character, location, dialogue, prop, vehicle, route, image, recording, time sequence, asset, code, file structure, or published Skill is reproduced. The ownership model, six locks, sight-zone graph, glance-budget method, stop and interference contracts, verification matrix, template, and fictional example were written for this repository.

**Calder Tunnel Washer** is wholly fictional and does not describe an existing game or implementation.

## Repository-authored contributions

- independent vehicle, road-demand, cabin-zone, and threat state;
- design-time glance allowance tied to observable route pressure;
- separate moving, slowing, stopped, and disabled threat permissions;
- stop nodes that trade inspection safety against exposure or schedule;
- common-sense responses for control interference;
- collision feedback separated from hostile interference;
- save-safe camera and control transition boundaries;
- comfort and accessibility equivalents that preserve decisions;
- one-ending arrival contract with vehicle-failure fallback.
