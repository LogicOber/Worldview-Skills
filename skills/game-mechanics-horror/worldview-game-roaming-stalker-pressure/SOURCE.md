## Original synthesis decisions

This package independently establishes that:

1. A roaming stalker remains one persistent actor with a recoverable location and history across streaming and save/load.
2. A pressure director may schedule an opportunity but cannot grant perception, movement, or attacks that the stalker's own rules forbid.
3. Zone graphs and connectors describe physical and declared abstract travel; materialization must preserve that route.
4. Visible, recently inspected, occupied, doorway, safe, unreachable, and no-counterplay physical staging or materialization candidates are hard rejections. An unloaded destination also rejects physical staging and materialization, but a declared abstract connector route may traverse unloaded zones under the locked abstract-travel policy.
5. Warning, encounter, counterplay, withdrawal, and recovery form a bounded grammar.
6. “No legal encounter” is a successful scheduling outcome, not an error that justifies an unfair fallback.
7. Recovery is required to restore anticipation and cannot accumulate punitive debt during refuge or accessibility use.

These choices remain project-specific proposals until implemented and tested in a named runtime.
