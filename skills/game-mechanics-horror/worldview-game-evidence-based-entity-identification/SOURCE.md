# Source and originality record

## Commission

`worldview-game-evidence-based-entity-identification` was authored for this repository in response to the user’s 2026-09-18 request for original, detailed, all-English Agent Skills that express classic horror-game play patterns. It is intended to load directly in Claude and Codex through a valid `SKILL.md` and stable Slash invocation.

## Originality boundary

No external Skill, named game, character, published entity taxonomy, commercial level, guide, or distinctive external wording was used. The method begins from a general design problem: a player must identify a dangerous unknown through repeatable observations without the game reducing evidence to arbitrary collectibles or secretly reading the answer into the notebook.

The **Wrenfall Conservatory** example, candidate names, instruments, evidence matrix, and setting were created for this repository. They do not record an existing implementation and must remain labeled as proposals until built.

## Problem reconstructed for this Skill

The Skill addresses several linked failures common to an identification loop:

- candidates may share indistinguishable signatures;
- evidence may appear without a valid test condition;
- the absence of a result may be treated as negative evidence too early;
- random withholding may make a case unsolvable;
- environmental contamination may look like arbitrary contradiction;
- the journal may know more than the player observed;
- danger may interrupt tests without declaring whether the result remains valid;
- multiplayer clients may disagree about case identity or accepted findings.

The package turns these failures into explicit candidate, protocol, ledger, authority, recovery, and verification contracts.

## New decisions introduced here

Original decisions include:

- the public name `/worldview-game-evidence-based-entity-identification`;
- a static solvability analysis for the candidate matrix;
- separation of identity, evidence capability, current behavior, ledger, and hypothesis;
- the preparation/eligibility/window/positive/negative/inconclusive/contaminated/retry protocol;
- provenance-bearing, idempotent evidence events;
- distinct observed, interpreted, and hypothesized notebook layers;
- explicit handling of test interruption and case versioning;
- a concrete post-identification action that closes the loop;
- accessibility requirements for each result class;
- multiplayer authority that replicates findings without leaking the hidden answer;
- the verification sequence and default handoff.

These are implementation and design proposals. They are not claims about every investigation game or scientific sensing process.
