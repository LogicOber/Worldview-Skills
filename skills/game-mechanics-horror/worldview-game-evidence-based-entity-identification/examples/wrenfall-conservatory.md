## Test protocols

### Condensation inversion

The player closes one small propagation cabinet, activates its cool mist, and waits inside the marked observation band while the presence crosses the adjacent greenhouse lane. A capable presence makes droplets retreat from the glass toward the frame.

Four perimeter moisture tabs measure the same movement and present `CENTER DRYING — EDGE GAIN` as scalable text, an outward segmented indicator, optional focus speech, and a center-to-edge haptic sweep. These are equivalent expressions of the accepted sample, not a second source of evidence.

- Positive: sustained outward-clearing pattern during the valid crossing.
- Valid negative: a complete crossing occurs with calibrated mist and no inversion.
- Inconclusive: cabinet opened early, no complete crossing, or the player leaves before observation commits.
- Contaminated: boiler exhaust enters through an open service damper and warms the glass unevenly.

The boiler contamination produces an asymmetric warm edge and airflow from the service side unlike the presence’s symmetric inversion. The instrument labels it `SERVICE-SIDE HEAT GRADIENT`, with a one-sided indicator and haptic pulse, so recognition does not depend on amber color or droplet vision. Closing the damper creates a clean retry.

### Delayed reflected motion

The player aligns a three-panel reflection frame across a known passage and observes while the presence crosses its field. A capable presence leaves one panel’s silhouette moving after the world-space crossing ends.

Each panel also owns a calibrated optical-motion tab. An accepted delayed sample names the panel and interval through scalable text such as `PANEL 2: MOTION +0.8 S`, optional focus speech, and a two-part haptic pulse whose separation represents the same delay. Players are not required to perceive reflected motion alone.

- Positive: delayed panel motion tied to the accepted crossing event.
- Valid negative: all three panels complete a stable crossing without delay.
- Inconclusive: alignment broken, panel occluded, or threat forces the player to collapse the frame.
- Contaminated: none in this compact case; ordinary player reflections are filtered through the project’s existing ownership tags.

### Resonant-wire harmonic

The player stretches a sensor wire between two authored anchors and primes it. A capable presence crossing the field adds a stable third harmonic shown by three separated notches and a captioned pulse pattern.

- Positive: harmonic persists for the minimum stable sample after a valid crossing.
- Valid negative: calibrated wire records a full crossing without the harmonic.
- Inconclusive: one anchor breaks, the wire is not primed, or observation ends before stability.
- Contaminated: none proposed; adding random noise would not improve this three-candidate case.

## Pressure and retreat

The presence patrols greenhouse lanes and enters an agitated state after an instrument is prepared. A leaf-rattle cue, high-contrast lane light, and caption announce its approach. The player can abandon an active test and retreat through a side door. Already accepted evidence persists; the current incomplete window becomes inconclusive.

No pressure state changes the selected identity or evidence capabilities. After the presence leaves the lane, instruments can be reset and the opportunity recreated.

## Identification consequence

At the potting station, the player selects one candidate and commits. A correct identification unlocks that candidate’s seal preparation. For `glass_root`, the player rotates three mineral shutters to interrupt reflected continuity, then crosses the conservatory exit boundary.

A submission is one irreversible commitment per encounter attempt and is enabled only after the ledger contains two accepted positives and one completed valid-negative opportunity. A wrong answer starts an incompatible procedure, produces an eight-second multimodal instability warning, and lets the player retreat before the case ends in containment failure. Retry preserves the candidate matrix but clears accepted case evidence, instrument samples, submission, seal state, and threat outcome while retaining the same hidden identity. The player must perform valid tests again before another submission; the UI never cycles candidates, reveals the answer, or changes the hidden identity. This cost prevents rapid menu brute force while keeping the reasoning reproducible.
