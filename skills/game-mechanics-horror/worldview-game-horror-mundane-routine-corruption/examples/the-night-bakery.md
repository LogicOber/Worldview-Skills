# Example: The Night Bakery

An original fictional example. Invoke it with `/worldview-game-horror-mundane-routine-corruption` when a filled routine contract is useful. The player learns to weigh flour, stamp a receipt, and place a loaf in the warm cabinet. On the fourth order the receipt has no customer name and the cabinet opens from the inside. Serving it keeps the street lights on but changes the next customer; inspecting it reveals a wet footprint and closes the front door; refusing it leaves the order on the counter and opens a rear route. All paths converge on the same ending, but the player's knowledge and recovery position differ.
