# Why mundane routines fail

## The baseline exists only in a cutscene

The player cannot compare a later violation against an action they never performed. Let the player create or confirm the normal state.

## Repetition has no later owner

Walking between markers is not meaningful work. A routine should teach a route or affordance, prove a relationship, create evidence, establish a promise, or place a later resource.

## The task is too complex to master

If the player is still decoding the normal recipe when it changes, error and anomaly look identical. Reduce steps until the normal confirmation is predictable.

## A fixed count replaces a mastery test

Three cycles are not automatically enough or necessary. Observe whether the player can select the station, perform the verb, and predict the result without correction.

## Optional teaching creates mandatory punishment

Players who skip a side demonstration need another fair comparison before a consequential violation. Otherwise exploration style becomes a hidden difficulty setting.

## Random anomaly stacking destroys inference

Changing actor, object, destination, sound, and result together creates spectacle but no testable question. Change one field, preserve anchors, then combine learned fields later.

## The anomaly appears only in dialogue

A narrator saying “that order is wrong” removes observation and verification. Put the contradiction in inspectable actor, object, route, time, or confirmation state.

## Forced blind time becomes a cheap attack

A task that locks view can create fair unseen change only after the before-state and warning rules are known. Leave residue and preserve a response window.

## Every response returns to the same state

If accept, refuse, inspect, and delay only change a line of dialogue, agency is decorative. Preserve local route, evidence, relationship, resource, or schedule consequences even with one ending.

## Refusal always means death

Then compliance is the only real input. Price refusal through time, trust, access, or evidence instead of treating it as a trick answer.

## The loop resets without memory

Repeating mastered work after failure tests patience. Persist learned rules or changed routes, or compress solved labor.

## Presentation owns truth

An animation, label, or sound can lie or fail to load. Authoritative routine and object state must exist independently and drive presentation.

## Minimal acceptance test

A new player can perform the normal loop, predict confirmation, spot the exact changed field before commitment, take two meaningful responses, recover from one wrong reading, and reload without losing the causal history.
