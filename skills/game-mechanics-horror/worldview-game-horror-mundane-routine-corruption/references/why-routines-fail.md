# Why mundane routines fail

- **No normal baseline:** if the player never performs the ordinary task, the anomaly has no contrast.
- **Random exception:** the player cannot learn from a violation that has no visible cause or category.
- **Pure busywork:** clicking through orders or chores is not horror unless the task changes what the player knows or risks.
- **No refusal:** a routine becomes a real decision when the player can comply, inspect, refuse, delay, or leave.
- **Punishment without recovery:** after a bad service or inspection choice, preserve a readable consequence and a way to revise the plan.

Keep the first loop short. Teach the action, the confirmation, and the normal completion before corrupting one field.
