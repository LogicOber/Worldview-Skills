The Skill begins from a general game-design problem: an injury can create an unfinished, time-dependent treatment decision without turning real illness, infection, or disability into a moral or dehumanizing shorthand. It does not adapt a published medical system, horror transformation, character, or level.

## Medical and representation boundary

This is not a medical reference. Its state labels and treatment sequence are abstractions for designing fictional gameplay. Any project requesting real clinical claims must provide approved sources and qualified review; the Skill instructs the Agent to label unreviewed values as fictional rather than guessing.

Illness and disability are not treated as guilt, corruption, lack of personhood, or automatic monstrosity. That boundary is part of the mechanic's acceptance criteria, not optional editorial polish.

## Repository writing method

The presentation follows the user's supplied Writing 2 principles without copying their language. It restores the design problem, separates declared facts, observations, fictional proposals, and reviewed claims, keeps reasons and counterexamples beside rules, and requires direct evidence before concluding that behavior or representation works.

## New design decisions

This Skill introduces:

- the public invocation `/worldview-game-wounds-infection-and-treatment`;
- separate wound, contamination, infection, treatment, and recovery responsibilities;
- an abstract inspect–stabilize–treat–reassess loop that is explicitly not real medical instruction;
- a declared time-source requirement for progression;
- reduced-intensity presentation that retains complete state information;
- anti-ableist and anti-dehumanization acceptance checks;
- persistence, migration, accessibility, and multiplayer authority boundaries;
- the default four-file handoff under `gameplay/<treatment-loop-slug>/`;
- the fictional Glass Orchard example.

Exact state names, effects, resources, and timings are proposals until tested and, where realism is claimed, reviewed.

## Independence from sibling mechanics

This Skill works on its own. It may connect to inventory scarcity, routes, pursuit, or saving, but it does not require any sibling Skill. Treatment-resource ownership, condition persistence, and respectful presentation remain explicit here when mechanics are combined.
