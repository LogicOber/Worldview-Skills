## What this package adds

This original synthesis develops the short brief into an independent public Skill by adding:

- a public Slash invocation and explicit routing boundaries;
- a separation among observation, permission, and communication;
- a project-recovery and evidence-classification method;
- a declared observation predicate with sample-point, frustum, occlusion, and hysteresis decisions;
- simulation ordering that prevents one-frame movement and contact leaks;
- route-pressure reasoning, contact rules, accessibility, and optional multiplayer authority;
- a reusable mechanic contract, causal reference, verification plan, and original example.

These additions are editorial and technical judgments made for this repository. They are not represented as findings from the supplied archive.

The presentation follows the user's internal Writing 2 preference: restore the underlying problem before giving rules, keep facts separate from proposals and judgments, retain the reason for constraints, include counterexamples, and allow the reasoning to remain detailed when compression would make the method harder to apply.
