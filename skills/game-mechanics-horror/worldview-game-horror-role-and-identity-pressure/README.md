# Worldview Game — Horror Role and Identity Pressure

Make recognition, social position, appearance, or a claimed role into a fair horror decision without reducing the mechanic to random hallucination or a real-world diagnosis.

## Call this Skill

```text
/worldview-game-horror-role-and-identity-pressure

Design a first-person horror scene where the player must decide whether the person
at the station is an attendant, a witness, or an imitation. Give three stable
tests—ticket mark, route position, and repeated phrase—then let presentation,
lighting, and social pressure conflict with one test at a time.
```

Use it when a threat depends on who is recognized, trusted, authorized, or mistaken for someone else. Use perception-distortion when the core is unreliable cues; use this Skill when the core is a social or role decision.

## It delivers

- role and authority contracts;
- stable identity tests and contaminated evidence;
- social pressure, refusal, and assistance choices;
- presentation changes tied to state;
- fairness, privacy, accessibility, failure, and ending checks.

## How the Skill proceeds

It locks **Role and authority**, **Identity evidence**, **Presentation conflict**, **Social choices and safety**, **Runtime proof**, and **Handoff** in that order.
