# Why role and identity pressure fails

## Face-only guessing
Likeness is brittle, inaccessible, and often arbitrary. Use behavior, access, custody, schedule, and private play-derived knowledge.

## Appearance changes truth
A model or voice swap should not rewrite actor ID, permissions, or culpability. Keep presentation separate.

## Role equals innocence or guilt
A legitimate warden can misuse access; an imitation can help. Judge behavior and evidence, not the title.

## Every person may be fake
Universal doubt removes relationships and tests. Bound copying or replacement and preserve reliable anchors.

## Password trivia replaces history
A static phrase can be stolen. Combine private knowledge with context, custody, schedule, or a performed action.

## Permissions are decorative
If authority changes only dialogue, the social decision has no system consequence. Make access, records, commands, contacts, or transfers real.

## Betrayal uses an unknown power
A betrayal is causal when a trusted actor uses a known permission or breaks a known promise. A surprise power invented at reveal teaches nothing.

## Social pressure exists only in dialogue
Put it in routes, locked services, dependent people, deadlines, witnesses, and custody state.

## Correct choice is highlighted
A red outline or ominous sting collapses judgment into reflex. Let the player run a test and accept a cost.

## No false-positive recovery
If one mistaken accusation permanently blocks understanding without warning, caution becomes guessing. Preserve residue, witnesses, or appeal.

## Hallucination removes every anchor
Unreliable presentation still needs a declared truthful channel. Announce the replacement before commitment.

## Real-world traits mark danger
Accent, disability, diagnosis, identity, religion, ethnicity, or appearance must never be a shortcut for monstrosity or culpability.

## Save/load merges layers
Reloading a new face with old permissions, or old observer beliefs with new evidence, creates accidental deception. Persist and reconstruct each layer explicitly.

## Minimal acceptance test
The player can name the role claim, permission, two fair tests, contaminated cue, social cost, false-positive recovery, and resulting state. Runtime actor truth and presentation remain consistent across reload.
