# Why role and identity pressure fails

- **Face guessing:** likeness alone is not a fair test; give stable behavioral or spatial evidence.
- **Random unreliability:** change one presentation layer at a time and preserve an anchor.
- **Social punishment without agency:** let the player ask, refuse, verify, or seek help.
- **Diagnosis metaphor:** do not map a real mental-health condition onto moral danger or “being less real.”
- **No consequence:** recognition must change access, route, trust, threat knowledge, or recovery.
