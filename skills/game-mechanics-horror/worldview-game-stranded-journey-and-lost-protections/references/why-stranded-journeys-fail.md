# Why stranded journeys and lost protections fail

## Isolation is a state transition, not an opening sentence

Saying the protagonist is alone does not create dependency. Establish which protections exist and let the player use them. When mobility, communication, shelter, navigation, credibility, or human assistance fails later, the player can name what changed and choose a response.

Remove protections separately. Simultaneous vehicle failure, phone loss, map loss, and shelter lock looks like author intervention unless one causal event visibly explains all four.

## Travel must change obligation

Long corridors and roads do not become meaningful because they are dark. Every route leg should alter the player's current obligation, retained protection, known threat access, evidence, help options, cargo exposure, or return cost. If nothing changes, compress or remove the leg.

## Help is not a binary morality prompt

A useful help offer has capability, authority, cost, and fallback. The player should know what control they yield: destination, privacy, item custody, time, route choice, or social obligation. Hidden danger can surprise once; repeated dependence needs symptoms or contradictory behavior.

Refusal must be playable. It may cost time, comfort, cargo integrity, or route safety, but it cannot be a silent dead end.

## Credibility has physical consequences only through actors

Being disbelieved does not magically close doors. A worker denies entry, a dispatcher deprioritizes a call, a companion refuses a detour, or an institution alters a record. Store actor, permission, observation, and action. This makes restoration possible through evidence or another authority.

## Familiar returns need a recorded contract

A return path becomes frightening because it previously worked. Record the earlier condition, not only the edge. A bridge crossed before the storm may still be unfamiliar under high water. Show a change before the no-return point and let the player prepare.

## Restoration is not a Boolean reset

A repaired radio may have short range. A reopened shelter may be monitored. A recovered vehicle may lack fuel. Record limitations and trust after restoration. Otherwise recovery erases decisions and the journey becomes a repeated on/off puzzle.

## Cargo creates pressure only when state is legible

Medicine temperature, evidence integrity, dependent health, or deadline must be visible enough to inform tradeoffs. Do not reveal at the ending that a rational detour secretly destroyed the obligation. Offer bounded warnings and more than one viable allocation.

## Group journeys are composition, not another owner

This method can own resource and route loss for a group. Task division, last-known companion state, communication acknowledgement, and AI fallback belong to asymmetric-information cooperation. Keep one owner for each record.

## A single ending does not erase choice

Acceptance, refusal, detour, damage, trust, and evidence can alter arrival procedure and aftermath while the destination remains fixed. This often produces denser consequences than manufacturing several endings that differ only in a final cutscene.

## Edited pacing cannot set journey length

Measure continuous travel, waiting, search, dialogue, repair, exposure, and recovery. A highlight edit can make a twenty-minute walk look like one beat. Verify first-time, lost, cautious, and repeated-failure durations in the build.
