# Why horror rhythm fails

## Constant intensity

If every room shouts, the player stops distinguishing danger from decoration. Keep a quiet beat long enough to learn a landmark or routine, then make the next pressure change one known variable.

## Empty quiet

Silence is not automatically tension. A quiet beat needs a question, a task, a route choice, or a new observation. Otherwise it is loading time disguised as atmosphere.

## Unpaid peaks

A loud image or pursuit creates a debt. Pay it with a changed rule, a resource cost, a new route, a relationship shift, or a reliable piece of knowledge. A recovery room that erases the debt makes the peak disposable.

## Recovery as a cutscene

Recovery should let the player reorganize, test a cue, or choose a next plan. A non-interactive speech panel may explain a story but does not restore agency.

## Source-time confusion

Edited footage can remove retries and walking. Keep source time, player time, and runtime event time separate. Mark an omitted interval unknown.
