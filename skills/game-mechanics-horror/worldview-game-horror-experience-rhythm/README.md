# Worldview Game — Horror Experience Rhythm

Design the timing of a horror experience so ordinary play, anomaly, pressure, cost, recovery, and payoff form a playable arc instead of a stack of jump scares.

## Call this Skill

```text
/worldview-game-horror-experience-rhythm

Shape the first 25 minutes of my game. Start with a quiet repair job, introduce
one contradiction, build toward a route decision and a short pursuit, then give
the player a recovery beat before the next chapter. Keep the ending single and
make every tension peak prove a player-facing rule.
```

Use it when the game has mechanics or rooms but the experience feels flat, rushed, exhausting, or like disconnected scenes. It owns pacing and attention contracts; it does not replace a chase, sound, puzzle, or save contract.

## It delivers

- a dual-clock experience timeline;
- a beat and attention budget;
- pressure, cost, recovery, and payoff contracts;
- camera, audio, lighting, and interface cues tied to decisions;
- first-time, fail, recovery, and ending checks.

## How the Skill proceeds

It locks **Promise**, **Beat map**, **Attention contract**, **Recovery contract**, **Runtime proof**, and **Handoff** in that order.
